# Team ownership and integration

The final product may be completed by one student or a team of up to four. This
record communicates responsibility and oral-defense scope; it is not an
automatic grade calculation.

## Participants

List GitHub handles only. Do not include student IDs, grades, accommodations,
or private feedback.

## Shared core

Describe decisions, reviews, and integration work owned jointly by the team.

## Individual vertical slices

For each participant, record:

- the user or scientific need;
- owned interfaces and implementation;
- tests and reproducibility evidence;
- documentation and operational responsibility;
- relevant issues, pull requests, and reviews; and
- dependencies on another participant's work.

## Integration and conflict history

Summarize one consequential integration decision or conflict. Explain the two
competing intentions, the evidence used, and the resulting contract.

## Maintenance responsibility

State who investigates defects in each major component through final review.
Authorship does not end when code first merges.
