Part III · In development

Unsupervised Learning Systems

Materials forthcoming

Part III will study how data reveal structure when no target label is supplied. The emphasis will remain systemic: mathematical objectives, representation choices, validation without ordinary ground truth, scalable computation, and deployment consequences.

  • Clustering and mixture-based views of latent structure
  • Dimensionality reduction, matrix factorization, and embeddings
  • Manifold, density, and anomaly-detection methods
  • Representation learning and applications to scientific discovery

How to use the materials

Read, investigate, then engineer.

Begin with the assigned textbook section. Use the notebook to vary assumptions and inspect evidence. Move durable behavior into modules, tests, scripts, services, and monitored workflows. The weekly announcement determines which units belong to a particular class meeting.
