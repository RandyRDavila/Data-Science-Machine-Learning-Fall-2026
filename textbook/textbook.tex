\chapter*{Where the book goes next}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Where the book goes next}
Parts I through III establish the computational systems foundation, a complete
supervised-learning arc from prediction contract to monitored release, and the
opening mathematical theory of unsupervised representation. Later chapters will
develop clustering, mixture models, manifold methods, anomaly detection, and
graph learning in depth before returning to modern applications and scientific
discovery. Each part preserves the same standard: mathematical argument,
executable investigation, reusable software, and evidence about operation.
