\chapter*{Where the book goes next}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Where the book goes next}
Parts I and II establish the computational systems foundation and a complete
supervised-learning arc from prediction contract to monitored release. A later
part will develop unsupervised and representation-learning methods, followed by
modern applications and scientific discovery. Each part will preserve the same
standard: mathematical argument, executable investigation, reusable software,
and evidence about operation.
